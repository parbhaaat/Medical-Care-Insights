# 🏥 Medical care insights Dashboard

An interactive Healthcare Analytics Dashboard built using **SQL** and **Power BI** to analyze hospital operations, patient demographics, billing trends, insurance performance, and clinical insights.

This project focuses on transforming raw healthcare data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, SQL analysis, KPI generation, and interactive dashboard visualization.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to help healthcare organizations:

- Monitor hospital revenue trends
- Analyze patient demographics
- Track admission patterns
- Evaluate insurance provider performance
- Identify high-cost medical conditions
- Monitor abnormal test results
- Improve operational efficiency

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of healthcare performance using interactive visualizations and business-focused KPIs.

# 📊 Key KPIs & Business Problems Solved

## 1. Highest Billing Medical Condition
### Business Problem
Hospitals need to identify which diseases generate the highest treatment costs to improve budgeting and financial planning.

### Insight
Cancer generated the highest hospital billing amount.

---

## 2. Insurance Provider Covering Most Patients
### Business Problem
Hospitals must evaluate which insurance providers handle the highest patient volume to strengthen insurance partnerships.

### Insight
Medicare handled the highest number of patient claims.

---

## 3. Age Group With Highest Admissions
### Business Problem
Understanding which age groups are admitted most frequently helps hospitals improve healthcare planning and specialized services.

### Insight
Senior citizens accounted for the highest hospital admissions.

---

## 4. Doctors Handling Most Patients
### Business Problem
Hospitals need to monitor doctor workload distribution to improve staffing efficiency and reduce overload.

### Insight
Top-performing doctors handled significantly more patients compared to others.

---

## 5. Average Billing by Admission Type
### Business Problem
Analyzing billing across admission types helps hospitals understand treatment cost patterns.

### Insight
Urgent admissions showed higher average billing costs.

---

## 6. Most Frequently Used Medication
### Business Problem
Tracking medication usage helps hospitals manage pharmaceutical inventory efficiently.

### Insight
Ibuprofen was the most frequently used medication.

---

## 7. Abnormal Test Result Analysis
### Business Problem
Hospitals need to identify diseases with high abnormal test cases for better risk monitoring.

### Insight
Certain medical conditions showed significantly higher abnormal test results.

---

## 8. Hospital Stay Duration Analysis
### Business Problem
Monitoring patient stay duration helps optimize bed management and operational planning.

### Insight
Hospital stay duration varied significantly across patients.
